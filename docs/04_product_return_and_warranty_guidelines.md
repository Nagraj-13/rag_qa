# Product Return, Exchange, & Warranty Guidelines

## 1. Hardware Return & Exchange Policy

### 30-Day Return Window
Customers may return hardware devices (e.g. Antigravity AI Edge Gateways, On-Prem Vector Appliances) within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund or exchange, provided:
1. The hardware is in original condition with all packaging, power cables, and documentation included.
2. The serial number matches customer purchase records.
3. No physical damage or unauthorized software tampering has occurred.

### Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Process
1. Contact customer support via `support@antigravity.ai` or submit a ticket to request an RMA number.
2. Our team will issue a prepaid shipping label within 24 hours.
3. Pack the device securely and attach the RMA label to the exterior of the box.
4. Refunds are processed within 3 to 5 business days after inspection at our distribution facility.

---

## 2. Hardware & Software Warranty Coverage

### 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty
All Antigravity Edge hardware products come standard with a 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty covering material and manufacturing defects.

### What is Covered:
- Component failure (motherboard, memory modules, neural accelerator cards).
- Power supply unit failures.
- Factory assembly defects.

### What is Not Covered:
- Liquid damage, drops, fire, or extreme physical impact.
- Unauthorized hardware modifications or opening of sealed enclosure chassis.
- Voltage surges or use with non-certified third-party power adapters.
