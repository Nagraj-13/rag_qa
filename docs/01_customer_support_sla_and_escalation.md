# Enterprise Customer Support SLA & Escalation Guidelines

## 1. Overview & Service Level Commitments
Antigravity RAG Enterprise provides 24/7 technical and operational support to ensure max operational uptime for support teams and internal knowledge bases.

### Priority Level & Target Initial Response Times (SLA)
- **Priority 1 (P1 - Critical Outage)**: System down or core AI RAG service unresponsive for all users.
  - **Target Initial Response**: Under 15 Minutes
  - **Target Resolution Time**: Under 2 Hours
  - **Support Coverage**: 24/7/365 Dedicated Phone & High-Priority Channel

- **Priority 2 (P2 - Major Degraded Service)**: Core features impacted, severe latency (>2000ms), or single LLM provider total rate limit.
  - **Target Initial Response**: Under 1 Hour
  - **Target Resolution Time**: Under 6 Hours
  - **Support Coverage**: 24/7 Email & Slack Connect Channel

- **Priority 3 (P3 - Minor Issue)**: Individual user access issues, document parsing errors, minor UI bugs.
  - **Target Initial Response**: Under 4 Business Hours
  - **Target Resolution Time**: Under 24 Hours
  - **Support Coverage**: Standard Business Hours (Monday–Friday, 8 AM – 8 PM EST)

- **Priority 4 (P4 - General Inquiry / Feature Request)**: Questions regarding API customization, model router setup, or document category organization.
  - **Target Initial Response**: Under 12 Business Hours
  - **Target Resolution Time**: Best Effort / Roadmap Planning

---

## 2. Support Channels & Access Points
1. **In-App Live Chat Assistant**: Available 24/7 via the RAG Portal with verified knowledge citations.
2. **Dedicated Support Portal**: `https://support.antigravity.ai` for submitting P1–P4 tickets.
3. **Emergency Hotline**: +1 (800) 555-0199 (Strictly reserved for Enterprise P1 Outages).
4. **Slack / Teams Shared Channels**: Available for Enterprise Tier customers with designated Technical Account Managers (TAMs).

---

## 3. Escalation Matrix & Management Contacts

If a ticket exceeds target resolution times or requires tier-3 engineering intervention, use the following escalation ladder:

- **Tier 1 (L1 Support Specialist)**: Triage, initial log collection, basic troubleshooting.
- **Tier 2 (L2 Systems & RAG Engineer)**: Model router config, Supabase pgvector debugging, rate-limit failovers.
- **Tier 3 (L3 Lead AI Architect & Infrastructure Lead)**: Core API outage, custom embedding model fixes, database RPC failure.
- **Executive Escalation**:
  - Escalation Officer: Support Director (`support-escalations@antigravity.ai`)
  - Vice President of Engineering (`vp-eng@antigravity.ai`)
