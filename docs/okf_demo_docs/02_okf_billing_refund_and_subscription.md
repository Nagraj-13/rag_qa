# Subscription Billing, Refund & Cancellation Policy

## 📖 Executive Summary
Comprehensive policy outlining subscription tiers, 30-day money-back guarantee, and data retention rules compliant with GDPR and SOC2 standards.

## 💡 Core Knowledge & Policy Specifications
- **Subscription Plans**:
  - **Developer / Starter ($29/month)**: Up to 50 uploaded documents & 10k RAG queries.
  - **Pro Team ($149/month)**: Up to 500 uploaded documents & 100k RAG queries.
  - **Enterprise Custom**: Unlimited documents, 99.95% SLA & dedicated model router.
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: 100% full refund within 30 days of initial purchase if technical issues remain unresolved or system uptime drops below 99.0%.
- **Cancellation & Data Purge**: Customers may cancel anytime via the portal. Access remains active through the billing period. All vector embeddings and files are permanently purged 30 days post-cancellation.
