# Subscription Billing, Refund, & Cancellation Policy

## 1. Plan Tiers & Billing Structure
Antigravity RAG offers flexible subscription tiers tailored for startups, growing teams, and global enterprises.

### Subscription Plans
- **Developer / Starter Plan ($29/month or $290/year)**:
  - Up to 50 uploaded documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT).
  - 10,000 vector RAG queries per month.
  - Standard Smart AI Router with Groq & Gemini free fallbacks.
  - Community & email support (24h response time).

- **Pro Team Plan ($149/month or $1,490/year)**:
  - Up to 500 uploaded documents.
  - 100,000 vector RAG queries per month.
  - Full Smart Router + Round-Robin LLM failover.
  - Priority support (4h response time SLA).

- **Enterprise Custom Plan (Custom Pricing / Annual Contract)**:
  - Unlimited documents & unlimited vector queries.
  - Custom Supabase pgvector instance & dedicated model router.
  - 99.95% Uptime SLA & 15-minute emergency phone response.
  - Dedicated Technical Account Manager & Slack Connect.

## 2. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee & Refund Eligibility

### Full Refund Eligibility Conditions
Customers are eligible for a **100% full refund** within 30 days of initial purchase under the following circumstances:
1. **Unresolved Technical Failure**: If the RAG Knowledge Assistant fails to parse or index uploaded documents due to system failure that our support team cannot resolve within 5 business days.
2. **Service Outage Violation**: If system availability drops below 99.0% during the first 30 days of the subscription.
3. **Accidental Renewal**: If an annual renewal occurs and a refund request is submitted within 7 days of the renewal charge (provided query usage is under 1,000 queries during that period).

### Non-Refundable Items
- Custom engineering setup fees for dedicated enterprise deployments.
- Overage query fees incurred beyond standard plan quotas.
- Accounts terminated due to terms-of-service violations or abuse.

---

## 3. Account Cancellation & Data Retention Policy
- Customers may cancel their subscription at any time directly through the Billing Portal or by contacting `billing@antigravity.ai`.
- Upon cancellation, access remains active until the end of the current billing cycle.
- **Data Export & Deletion**: Customers have 30 days post-cancellation to export uploaded documents and query analytics. After 30 days, all vector embeddings and uploaded files are permanently scrubbed from database storage in accordance with GDPR and SOC2 standards.
