# Product Return, Warranty & Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Guide

## 📖 Executive Summary

Antigravity Technologies offers a **30-day no-questions-asked return window** for all hardware products and a **1-year limited manufacturer's warranty** covering defects in materials and workmanship. All return and warranty processes comply with the **FTC Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act** and applicable state consumer protection laws.

---

## 💡 Return Policy — 30-Day Return Window

### Eligibility

| Condition | Eligible for Return? | Refund Type |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------|
| **Unopened / Sealed** (within 30 days) | ✅ Yes | 100% full refund |
| **Opened / Used but Defective** (within 30 days) | ✅ Yes | 100% full refund or free replacement |
| **Opened / Used, No Defect** (within 30 days) | ✅ Yes | 85% refund (15% restocking fee) |
| **Beyond 30 days, Manufacturer Defect** | ⚠️ Warranty claim only | Free repair or replacement |
| **Beyond 30 days, No Defect** | ❌ No | Not eligible |
| **Physical Damage / Misuse** | ❌ No | Not eligible |

### Return Process

1. **Initiate Return**: Email `returns@antigravity.ai` or call +1 (888) 555-ANTI ext. 200 with your order number.
2. **Receive RMA Number**: An RMA authorization number will be issued within 1 business day.
3. **Package & Ship**: Securely package the product in its original packaging (if available). Attach the **prepaid shipping label** provided by our logistics team.
4. **Inspection & Refund**: Upon receipt, our Quality Assurance team inspects the unit within 2 business days. Refund is processed to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after inspection approval.

### Non-Returnable Items

- Custom-configured enterprise hardware appliances (built-to-order)
- Software license keys (once activated)
- Consumable accessories (cables, adapters) opened from sealed packaging

## 🛡️ Warranty Coverage — 1-Year Limited Warranty

### What's Covered

| Component | Coverage Period | Coverage Details |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Main Processing Unit** | 12 months | Manufacturer defects, power supply failures, internal component faults |
| **Display / Screen** | 12 months | Dead pixels (>5), backlight failure, touch digitizer malfunction |
| **Battery** | 6 months | Capacity degradation below 80% of rated capacity |
| **Accessories (Charger, Cables)** | 6 months | Manufacturer defects only |

### What's NOT Covered

- Damage from drops, liquid spills, or physical impact
- Unauthorized modifications, repairs, or disassembly
- Cosmetic wear (scratches, dents) that does not affect functionality
- Software issues (covered under software support SLA, not hardware warranty)
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers

### Warranty Claim Process

1. **Contact Support**: Email `warranty@antigravity.ai` with your serial number and a description (+ photos) of the defect.
2. **Diagnostic Assessment**: Our Tier 2 engineer may request remote diagnostics or a short video demonstrating the issue.
3. **RMA Issuance**: If warranty-eligible, an RMA number and prepaid return label are issued.
4. **Repair or Replace**: Depending on the defect, we will either repair the unit (target: 5–7 business days) or ship a replacement unit within 3 business days.
5. **Advance Replacement (Enterprise Only)**: Enterprise customers receive a replacement unit shipped before returning the defective unit.
