# Customer Technical Support & Troubleshooting FAQ Guide

## 1. Account & System Access Issues

### How do I reset my account password?
If you are locked out or forgot your account password:
1. Go to the Sign In page and click **"Forgot Password"**.
2. Enter your registered account email address.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset verification link.
4. Click the link within 15 minutes and create a new password containing at least 10 characters, one uppercase letter, one number, and one special symbol.

### What should I do if my account is locked?
Accounts are automatically locked for **15 minutes** after 5 consecutive failed login attempts to protect against unauthorized access. You can either wait 15 minutes for the lockout to expire or click **"Unlock Account via Email"** on the login screen.

## 2. Product Setup & Connection Troubleshooting

### Why am I experiencing high latency or slow response times?
Slow response times or latency spikes exceeding 2000ms are typically caused by transient network congestion or temporary model provider rate limit cooldowns. Our system automatically switches to fallback providers within seconds. If slow response times persist past 5 minutes, please clear your browser cache or switch to an alternate internet connection.

### How do I connect to customer support via Slack Connect?
Pro and Enterprise plan customers receive dedicated Slack Connect channel invitations upon subscription activation:
1. Log in to your Account Dashboard and navigate to **Support -> Slack Connect**.
2. Enter your team's Slack workspace domain.
3. Accept the channel invitation sent to your administrator's email address.

## 3. General Support & SLA Inquiries

### What are customer support hours of operation?
- **P1 Critical Outage Support**: 24/7/365 availability for all Enterprise and Pro plans via Slack Connect and emergency phone lines.
- **Standard Technical Support**: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST via email and support portal tickets.

### How do I request a feature enhancement?
Feature requests and non-urgent suggestions can be submitted via the Support Portal under **Submit Ticket -> Request Feature**. Our product management team reviews all feature requests weekly.
