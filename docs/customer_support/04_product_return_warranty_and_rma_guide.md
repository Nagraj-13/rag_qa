# Product Return, Hardware Warranty & RMA Guidelines

## 📖 Executive Summary
Comprehensive return policy, limited 1-year hardware warranty specifications, and Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) procedures fully compliant with the US FTC Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act and EU Consumer Rights Directives.

---

## 📦 Return & Exchange Window
- **30-Day Return Window**: Customers may return unopened, undamaged, or defective hardware items within **30 days of delivery** for a 100% full refund or direct unit replacement.
- **Return Condition Requirements**: Returned items must include original packaging, power adapters, cables, and accessories.
- **Restocking Fees**: No restocking fees are charged for defective items or returns requested within 14 days of delivery.

---

## 🔧 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty
All hardware devices and edge deployment units supplied by Antigravity Enterprise carry a **1-Year Limited Warranty** covering manufacturing defects:

- **Covered Deficiencies**: Internal motherboard component failure, power supply module failure, network interface port defects, and firmware corruption under normal operational usage.
- **Exclusions**: Damage caused by physical drops, liquid exposure, unauthorized modification, electrical power surges, or environmental abuse.

---

## 🚚 Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Process

1. **Step 1 — Request RMA**: Contact customer support via ticket or email `rma@antigravity.ai` with product serial number and failure description.
2. **Step 2 — Receive Prepaid Shipping Label**: Upon validation, our logistics team issues an official RMA tracking code and prepaid shipping label within 4 hours.
3. **Step 3 — Package & Ship**: Securely package the unit and drop off at any authorized shipping carrier location.
4. **Step 4 — Fast Replacement**: Once the carrier scans the return package, a brand-new replacement unit is dispatched via expedited 2-day air shipping.
